Questions

Can Facebook block my account?

Facebook limits how fast accounts can do some actions and doesn't publish those limits. Friend List Cleaner removes friends slowly, with random pauses and a daily limit, and stops if Facebook shows a warning. That lowers the risk a lot, but no tool can remove it completely.

Does it read or send my Facebook data?

It reads what's on your friends list only so it can show checkboxes and the review list. Nothing about your friends leaves your computer. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I undo a removal?

No. Removing a friend is permanent, which is why the extension shows you the full list before it starts. You can send a new friend request afterwards.

Which languages does it support?

It works best with Facebook set to English. Other languages are being added.

How do I get Pro?

Click "Get Pro" in the extension's panel. You pay once through Lemon Squeezy and receive a license key by email. Paste it into the extension and Pro turns on straight away.

Is this made by Facebook?

No. Friend List Cleaner is made by Star Labs Ceylon and is not affiliated with Meta.
