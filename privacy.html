Privacy Policy

Friend List Cleaner by Star Labs Ceylon · Last updated 5 November 2026

Friend List Cleaner ("the extension") is a Chrome extension made by Star Labs Ceylon. It helps you remove friends from your own Facebook friends list.

What the extension accesses

The extension only runs on Facebook when you click its toolbar icon while a Facebook page is open. It then reads what is already shown on that page (your friends' names and mutual-friend counts) so it can:

  • show checkboxes next to each friend,
  • select friends by mutual-friend count,
  • list the friends you selected so you can review them before anything is removed.

What the extension stores

Using Chrome's local extension storage, on your own computer only, it saves:

  • your settings (daily limit and mutual-friend threshold),
  • how many friends you removed today, so the daily limit works,
  • if you raise the daily limit above the recommended level: that you accepted the warning, up to which limit, and when,
  • if you activate Pro: your license key and Lemon Squeezy's reply about it (whether it's active, the plan name, the expiry date and the email address used at purchase), so the extension knows your plan.

It does not store your friends' names or any other information about your friends.

What the extension sends or shares

Your Facebook data is never sent anywhere. The extension has no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and loads no code from the internet.

The only data that leaves your computer is for Pro licenses. When you activate Pro, and every few days after that, the extension sends your license key and a device label (for example "Chrome on Windows") to Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), our payment provider, to check that the key is valid. Chrome asks your permission before the first check. Nothing is sent if you use the free plan.

Purchases happen on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page and are covered by Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy. The extension never sees your payment details.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. To free your Pro activation first, open the extension's Pro page and click "Remove from this computer".
